# mag-4 Route B: walkthrough, Parts 5 (tail) to 8 (end)

Sources: [A] = "guide-09" (two-page spread; left page number not legible, right page printed "50"). [B] = "guide-10" (second spread, Part 8, page numbers not visible). All facts paraphrased in English. Guide order kept.
Note: the very top of [A] starts mid-section of Part 5/6 (heading not visible); Part 7 and Part 8 headings are visible.

## [A, left page] Tail end of the previous part (Patrick's Quarters area)
- Beating the boss at the start of this column grants the Reflect spell. [A-left]
- Go to the water fountain; when leaving the room, Elaine appears and a conversation starts. After the film, return to the main room of Patrick's Quarters. [A-left]
- Go up the stairs and take the door at the top-left of the stairs into the next room. [A-left]

### 2F Study, Patrick's Quarters
- Go to the bookshelves ahead. There is a paper with a drawing of a dragon; it is a hint for the secret passage in the next room. [A-left]
- Cross the room, pick up Shotgun Shells, then leave through the door near the shells. [A-left]

### 2F Laboratory, Patrick's Quarters
- Search the shelves on the left side: Empty Bottle. [A-left]
- Go to the back of the room: Patrick's Memo on the table. [A-left]
- Walk around the table: Rifle Rounds. [A-left]
- Leave the way you came and return via the stairs to the main area of Patrick's Quarters. [A-left]

### 1F Staircase, Patrick's Quarters
- Go right of the stairs and climb more stairs to a door at the top. [A-left]

### 2F Hallway, Patrick's Quarters
- Take the door at the upper right of the corridor. After passing the corridor, go left and examine the statue on the wall. Moving the statue makes it vanish and reveals a hidden door, located on the left side of where the statue stood. [A-left]

### 2F Secret Library, Patrick's Quarters
- Roger is there researching, looking through a pile of books. After the talk, go to the Acid Tank and fill your empty bottle with acid. [A-left/mid]

### 1F Basement, Acid Tank
- Go to the tank and fill the bottle with acid. Then return to the Secret Library. [A-mid]

### 2F Secret Library (second visit)
- Roger is still researching. After a short exchange, leave for Patrick's Laboratory. [A-mid]

### 2F Laboratory, Patrick's Quarters (second visit)
- Go past the table to the shelf area at the back; near the shelf a trapdoor opens. Descend the stairs; you arrive in the underground passage beneath Patrick's Quarters. [A-mid]

### 1F Passageway, Beneath Patrick's Quarters
- Walk to the back of the room: Panacea on the floor. [A-mid]
- Puzzle: in the lower right corner of the room there are some steps. The text says to set a weight of 25 kg; the door only opens when the correct weight is placed. Then go through the door that unlocked. (Exact mechanic of "setting your weight" is unclear, see Unclear.) [A-mid]

## Part 7: Banishing the evil of the Unholy Church [A]
### 1F Ruins, Church Vestry
- You are in the Vestry Ruins. Take the Tinderbox, the Shotgun 6 and Shotgun Shells from the body. Then cross the bridge and take the door in the left wall. (Screenshot caption "You found Tinderbox".) [A-mid]

### 1F Altar, Church Vestry
- Go straight to the Altar. Take Roman Nuts and Pistol Rounds from the tables. [A-mid/right]
- Doll puzzle: the Altar has 6 dolls, four of which can be moved/removed. Press the X button to move the dolls and they come forward to the inner side. When all four dolls face inward, a door opens on the left. Go through the door that opens. (Caption: "You see four dolls facing each other. It looks like they can be moved.") [A]

### 1F Pulpit Room, C. Vestry
- Go to the center of the screen and pick up Shotgun Shells. After taking the item, go to the huge double doors. [A]

### 1F Hallway, Church Vestry
- Go through the door in the right wall. You are now at the Confessional. [A]

### 1F Conventional (sic), C. Vestry [likely the Confessional area]
- Go to the Altar at the back of the room and climb onto it. Light the candelabra with the Tinderbox; this reveals the Lifedrinker. Take the great sword and leave the way you came. [A]

### 1F Hallway, Church Vestry (before the Chapel)
- Take the door at the top of the hallway wall. Before entering, prepare your characters for a fierce battle. Raise the maximum level you can; rationale: the fight is hard and the next room has the fight. Fire! [A]
- Suggested stats (guide's recommendation): Koudelka magic level 3 and INT, MND, PIE above 60. Edward equipped with the Lifedrinker sword (the guide says to give it to Edward), STR around 80+. James magic level 2 and MND, INT, PIE around 60. Carry many Roman Nuts, Listel and Potion so you can heal quickly. [A-left/right]
- Tactic: Edward attacks with the Lifedrinker, use Geyser when the enemy is stunned/stopped. [A-right]

### 1F Chapel, Church Vestry: boss fight (Apostle)
- Go to the fountain; the battle starts. Put Edward at the front; James and Koudelka stay behind using Geyser and healing Edward whenever needed. [A-right]
- Trick: do not attack while the Apostle is moving; use that time to heal your group. Apostle HP is about 9000. [A-right]
- After the fight, save, leave through the door you came in by, and go to the Vestry Hallway. [A-right]

### 1F Hallway, Church Vestry (after the Apostle)
- Go left when leaving the chapel. A film starts; the sanctuary door is still locked and cannot be passed. James has the idea of blowing the door down with explosives. When the film ends the game switches to disc 4. Go back to Patrick's Laboratory. [A-right]

### 2F Laboratory, Patrick's Quarters (disc 4)
- James wants to be alone to prepare his mixtures while Edward and Koudelka drink by the fireplace. After some talk James will have finished his mixture. [A-right]

### 2F Study, Patrick's Quarters (choice point)
- You now choose: face the Gargoyle and get a Sacnoth Blade, or go to the chapel gates so James can break them down. [A-right]
- If you fight the Gargoyle, first raise Koudelka's magic skills. Ready? Go fight the Gargoyle, described as one of the hardest demons in the monastery. Return to the Inner Grounds via Patrick's Quarters, enter the Church; the Gargoyle waits there. [A-right]

### 1F Church Nave: boss fight (Gargoyle)
- Formation: Edward and James side by side in front, Koudelka behind acting as healer. Physical weapons are useless against it. Have Koudelka cast Geyser repeatedly; you will spend a long time on Geyser. Keep Koudelka and the others at good HP/MP while attacking. Bring plenty of healing items and do not be stingy. [A-right]
- Do not let the Gargoyle kill James or Edward, otherwise it quickly reaches Koudelka. Long, grueling battle. Gargoyle HP about 10000. Also use Reflect on your party since it can cast spells. [A-right]
- Reward: Sacnoth Blade, found near the main gate on a statue holding a sword. Very powerful weapon that makes the game much easier. If you need healing items, this is your last chance to get them (guide says it is the last chance to find them). [A-right]

### 1F Hallway, Church Vestry (breaking in)
- James places nitroglycerin on the door and blows it up with one shot. Door destroyed; enter the church through it and watch the film. [A-right]
- Go to the top of the screen and climb the stairs to the top. There is an organ; before touching it, look at the Old Letter in your inventory. Go to the instrument and read the runes on the keys. Press the keys in the order: Secret, Pain, People, Light. [A-right]
- Go back down the stairs and up to the altar in the center of the Church. The instrument's notes open a secret door. Go through the new door. [A-right]

### 1F Underground, Cauldron, Chapel
- You finally find Patrick decomposing; he was killed by his own experiments, in the cauldron. He was crushed to death by branches of the Tree of Life. When the film ends go to the Cauldron and throw Daniel's Arm in. [A-right]
- Koudelka escapes with the others while James throws the lantern at the tree (or something similar), leaving it burning. [A-right, partially legible]

## Part 8: Saving Elaine's soul [B]
### 2F Wall, Outer Spire
- Climb the stairs on Koudelka's left to the 2nd floor. Passing the 2nd floor, the Tree of Life attacks. Use the Flare spell and fire-based weapons. Koudelka stays behind casting; Edward uses the Shotgun on the tree. Easy compared to the Gargoyle. [B]
- Reward: AGL Idol, use it on Koudelka. Climb the stairs to the 3rd floor, pick up Shotgun Shells, continue. [B]

### 3F Outer Spire
- More tentacles attack on arrival. Same tactic. They have about 8000 HP each but go down quickly. [B]
- Rewards: INT Idol (give to James), STR Idol (give to Edward). Continue to the 4th floor. [B]

### Hidden Save Point and Pendant check
- Crossing this passage there is a hidden Save Point behind small vertical spirals. Save and continue up to the 4th floor. If you have the Pendant, you proceed; if not, you must fight the Black Cat to get it. You need the Pendant to beat Elaine. WARNING (repeated at 4F): without the Pendant the game ends there. [B]

### 4F Outer Spire
- Jump to the small platform and walk to the glass ahead; you see a lotus flower stuck to the tree. Koudelka senses something inside the plant. The plant reveals Elaine's deformed body, which attacks. Koudelka saves the day with the Pendant; Elaine goes crawling onto the ceiling like a spider. Edward shoots her, she falls but is not dead, and she follows the group to the next floor. [B]

### 5F Spiral Stairs, Inner Spire (boss fight 1)
- After the film, go on; climb to the fifth floor. As soon as you leave the stairs Elaine attacks first with strong weapons. Put Edward in front using an Evil Horn. Koudelka and James attack with fire magic like Flare. Protect the team with Reflect so Elaine's spells are bounced. HP about 8000. After winning, continue to the top of the spiral. Recharge HP and MP, one more fight is coming. [B]

### 6F Spiral Stairs, Inner Spire (boss fight 2)
- At the top of the stairs Elaine attacks again. Use Geyser and Megalith. All other aspects are as before: HP about 8000, use Reflect to prevent magic attacks. Equip Edward with Lifedrinker and finish her. Then go to the top of the tower, "prepare to die or live". [B]

### 7F Spiral Stairs, Inner Spire (final boss)
- At the top you witness the sunrise. Nothing is over: Elaine has transformed into a terrible mutant spider. As she screams the spire explodes, throwing fragments everywhere; the final battle starts. [B]
- Elaine is much stronger now. Cast Reflect on the whole party. Equip Edward with the Lifedrinker and put him in front to keep Koudelka from being attacked. HP about 10000. She will heal herself several times. Use Megalith on her for bigger damage and never stop attacking. Finish and the game is over; Elaine's spirit can rest. [B]

## The Ending [B]
- Date shown: 1 November 1898 (Tuesday), Nemeton Abbey, Aberystwyth, Wales. [B]
- Elaine's grotesque body explodes from the Megalith spell Koudelka casts. Elaine tries desperately to say some words but falls dead in the monastery. Her spirit is finally free; the monastery lies in ruins. Koudelka and team have cleansed it of all evil. A crying voice that had been calling Koudelka says the monastery is finally free. [B]
- James sits down to watch the sun rise and realizes he has gained nothing but memories. He forgets why he was sent there and that he failed to obtain a copy of the Emigre document for the Papacy. The mystical document is burning along with the rest of the sanctuary, so the Vatican can no longer bring humans back from death to build new civilizations, thanks to God. Roger Bacon, however, has not seen anything so exciting in 600 years. [B]

## Order summary
Patrick's Quarters study/lab/library/acid -> underground passage (weight door) -> Vestry Ruins (Tinderbox) -> Altar (dolls) -> Pulpit Room -> Confessional (Lifedrinker via candelabra) -> Chapel (Apostle ~9000 HP) -> locked sanctuary door, disc 4 -> optional Gargoyle (~10000 HP) for Sacnoth Blade -> nitroglycerin on door -> organ runes Secret, Pain, People, Light -> Cauldron (Daniel's Arm) -> Outer Spire (Tree of Life, tentacles ~8000, Idols, Pendant/Black Cat) -> Lotus/Elaine -> Elaine x3 (8000, 8000, 10000) -> ending.
