# Magazine guide 2 - reference facts (monsters, items, magic, strategy start, tips)

Source: three scans of a printed Brazilian Portuguese guide. All facts paraphrased in English.
File keys: [A] = Screenshot guide-04 (monsters page + items page), [B] = Screenshot guide-05 (magic page p.32 + strategy intro p.32 + walkthrough Part 1 p.34), [C] = Screenshot guide-11 (Koudelka Dicas, p.45). Cheat codes on [C] deliberately omitted.

## 1. Monsters and Evil Spirits [A, left page]
Names are as printed in the guide (English names). Advice is the guide's recommendation.

- Beads: floating lumps of flesh; easy, kill with magic or physical attacks.
- Black Cat: cats that cast strong HP-draining magic; kill with weapons.
- Brute: rises from graves/mounds; use Geyser to send it back.
- Chikon: human/animal genetic mishap; weak to magic and to fast physical hits; dark weapons do nothing to it.
- Crippled Giant: large ghost with boss-level HP; kill fast before it attacks; use Sacnoth Blade (spelled "Sacnoth Blade" in scan) or Megalith.
- Deformed Headless Cripple: throws shards of glass from its neck; runs at you and attacks with glass; use Flare and Megalith.
- Demonic Nobleman: fierce attacker with rapier swords, also casts strong magic; use Flare.
- Evil Scorpion: poison-claw attacker; a good weapon cuts it; magic also fine.
- Evil Chairs: possessed chairs; use Flare; the three chairs are not truly monsters (wording unclear).
- Evil Shelf: tough because of high durability; has poison attacks; use magic.
- Evil Table: paralysis-casting tables; use Geyser to turn them into sawdust.
- Eye Balls: surprisingly fast; high HP, takes several magic hits.
- Giant Roaches: easy prey; physical hit or low-level magic.
- Inbreds: floating deformed flesh balls; attack physically at close range but better at casting; needs plenty of experience/magic to beat.
- Inverse: headless deformed spirits dropping from the ceiling, attack with a pistol; mainly target Koudelka, often knocking her out of the battlefield; use Geyser.
- Joker: casters that mostly buff their own stats instead of dealing damage (rest of entry cut off/ILLEGIBLE).
- Knotted Flesh Ghost: kill with weapons; weak spells, low HP.
- Knights In Armor: use an attack spell; watch for the paralysis attacks on your party.
- Left Arm: severed arm crawling along the floor; more startling than dangerous; use a sharp weapon.
- Limbless Minotaur: hits hard; beat with powerful magic.
- Mannequin: floating dolls that cast fire; hit with any magic to defeat quickly.
- Marses: strange monsters with ropes on their legs; attack from the corners of the battlefield with magic; run to them and kill with a weapon.
- Mutilated Corpses: remains of humans wandering the Abbey, often missing head/arms/torso; attack with magic and with bones from their bodies; use spells to free them.
- Puppets: physically weak, destroyed by a good sword hit; do not waste magic.
- Raven: bird, about the most detestable enemy; use a weapon to finish it.
- Skeleton: attacks by bashing with its skull; use Geyser to send them back.
- Sucks: brothers of the Giant Roaches and faster; easy to kill, use physical attacks.
- Tamacoss: looks more annoying than it is; use Tornado.
- Viewer: huge floating eye that casts magic; high total HP, hard to beat; use physical attacks, magic only if needed.
- Winged Head: ugly demons attacking in groups; they cast magic; use weapons.
- Zombie Flesheater: slow walking dead; allows a quick kill with Flare; zombies attack physically.
- Zombie Hound: dogs faster than other zombies, attack before you can act; use fire weapons, with spells; tough, so keep attacking until they die.
- Zombie Female: quick, uses a fire weapon, kills like other horrible demons; slow to die; use Flare.
(An "Evil Scorpion" entry is printed without bold in the scan.)

## 2. Items [A, right page, headed "Itens"]
Clothing / armor (stat boosted when equipped):
- Rags: just another clothing layer; raises PIE; good for casting.
- Frock: priest garment, some magic protection; raises PIE and LUC.
- Robe: another priestly garment; raises PIE and LUC.
- Leather Mail: armor with protection, allows fast movement; raises VIT and LUC.
- Chain Mail: good protection; raises LUC and INT.
- P Mail: heavy armor, great protection, but hard to move in; raises VIT.

Accessories:
- Ring: silver ring with a star, keeps evil spirits away; raises PIE, INT, MND.
- J Ring: gold ring with a diamond, increases magic ability; raises INT, MND, PIE.
- Brooch: ruby brooch that raises magic defense; raises INT, MND, PIE.
- Badge: gold and silver medal giving protection; raises INT, MND, PIE.
- Jewel: silver necklace with pearls; raises PIE, MND, INT.
- Rosario: gold crucifix with sapphires, repels evil spirits; raises INT, PIE, MND.
- Flare Brooch: gold brooch with a ruby; raises VIT, INT, MND, PIE.
- Star Brooch: topaz brooch with an inlaid image; raises VIT, MND, AGL, PIE.
- Pendant: Koudelka's own pendant, radiates magic power; raises INT, PIE, MND, LUC. The guide says it is needed to defeat the final boss Elaine.

Ammo:
- Pistol Rounds: ammo for any pistol, 20 rounds.
- Rifle Rounds: ammo for any rifle, 20 rounds.
- Shotgun Shells: ammo for any shotgun, 20 rounds.
- Bowgun Arrows: ammo for any bowgun, 10 metal-tip arrows.

Consumables:
- Bread: heals 150 HP.
- Cheese: heals 250 HP.
- Dry Foods: heals 350 HP.
- Potion: heals 500 HP.
- High Potion: heals 1500 HP.
- Roman Nuts: restores all HP and MP.
- Listel: restores 40 MP.
- High Listel: restores 100 MP.
- Antidote: cures poison.
- Panacea: cures paralysis, silence and poison.
- Whiskey: revives a knocked-out character with 50 HP.
- Elixir: restores all MP and HP, but only on a conscious character.
- Scrolls: hold elemental magic; used in combat they cast a very effective spell on enemies.

## 3. Magic [B, p.32]
General system:
- Every character can cast; most spells are offensive, some are defensive or for healing/protecting the party.
- You will cast often as you explore the Abbey. MP is needed, and to have enough you need bonus AP put into the PIE score.
- Higher-level characters get more HP for casting; better to specialize a character in magic.
- Spells tie to four natural elements: Fire, Water, Earth, Air. As spell level rises its effect increases; spells can go up to level 3, which are the most effective.

Spell list (with how it is obtained):
- Revive: restores power in battle and revives (restores HP). Obtained after killing the Reflection demons in the Priests quarters.
- Flare: fire attack that burns enemies. Obtained by killing the Werewolf in the first battle.
- Geyser: water and ice attack that freezes enemies. Obtained after defeating the Planter.
- Tornado: wind and lightning attack causing great devastation. Obtained after defeating the dungeon guards (wording "vigias da dungeon").
- Megalith: earth attack, throws stones from the floor. Obtained after killing the Mummified Bride.
- Reflect: reflects a spell back at the caster. Obtained after killing the Scapegoat in Patrick's quarters. The strength of the person who reflects it decides the damage the enemy takes; essential for beating the game's bosses.
- Heal: uses MP to restore HP of other characters; can be cast outside combat from the Menu screen; the caster gains experience. If accidentally cast on a demon it recovers its HP.

## 4. Strategy Guide start [B, p.32 and p.34]
Intro [p.32]:
- Game opens with an intro movie: Koudelka enters the Monastery; inside she finds a man in agony, Edward.
- While she talks to Edward, the Werewolf appears and the first battle is forced. Edward throws her his pistol; the rest is up to the player.
- Normal battles are random; boss battles are fixed. The guide covers fixed battles only because random ones never repeat in the same place.
- In the early stages you must rely on weapons for your characters until they get magic powers.
- The strategy is split into eight parts according to the main story developments.

Part 1 - Saving Edward and Jones from the demons [p.34]:
- 2nd Floor, Grain Storage Room: have Koudelka shoot the Werewolf with the pistol; hit it three or four times and it dies. It makes a few attacks but no real damage. Winning rewards the Flare spell. After it dies, nothing else to do; go to where Edward lies and press X to talk. Because Edward saved her life by giving her the pistol, Koudelka heals Edward in return. After the cutscenes Edward joins Koudelka. Leave via the door behind Edward. Walk to the door and press X. Pick up the map on this room, above the barrel on the left when facing the top of the stairs. Also take Bread on the table at the back and leave the way you entered. (Order of events partly garbled in the scan; see Unclear.)
- 2nd Floor Hallway: go down the stairs and enter the door on the wall at the top of the right corner of the room. In the second-floor passage, walk to the ship painting on the wall and pick up a Dirk knife hidden behind it. Keep walking to the end of the hall and pick up the Listel potion behind the door, on the floor in front of you. Return through the hall and go downstairs to the first floor. The item screen shows "Monastery Map".
- Living Room: enter the first door on the left in the hallway of the living room. Inside you are warmly greeted by the Abbey guards Ogden and Bessy Hartman. Talk to the elderly couple and they offer hot soup. Koudelka passes the soup to Edward, who eats a little. Take the bullets the Hartmans give you and leave the room.
- 2nd Floor, Straw Storage: Koudelka is in the Caretaker's Quarters, 2nd floor. Ahead on the floor is a Potion; pick it up. Then go to the door in the center at the back of the screen and enter.
- 3rd Floor, Bedroom: go upstairs and pick up Pistol Rounds at the top of the stairs. Use X to climb the other stairs. (Rest of the route into the kitchen is unclear.)
- Kitchen: walk to the second door on the left and enter. Go down the stairs to the kitchen, walk to the stone and watch the film; Edward seems poisoned. Take the knife on the right side of the room, and take the cheese from the table. Leave through the door on the right-hand corner and enter the Well room.
- Well Room: walk to the stone and take a Panacea, then return to the well room. Looking down the well shows light coming from another level. Leave the well room the way you came.
- Forging Room: go back through the kitchen and take the other door at the top of the stairs; you should be in the Forging room. Take a Listel and the Hammer from the table and the Pipe from the floor. Leave through the door opposite the one you entered and go to the Herb Garden.
- Herb Garden: ignore the man lying on the ground for now. First you must face the Tree Demon. To beat it, have Koudelka cast Flare while Edward cuts the demon with a sharp knife. Place Edward directly in front of the boss to attack; if you attack from the sides the boss will defend. Koudelka can do great damage with Flare. Keep attacking until it falls, and keep Koudelka away from the demon's branches. (Entry continues past the page; ending cut off.)

## 5. Koudelka Dicas (tips page, p.45) [C]
General tips:
- Learn the game's spells; use the map to find where items are in the monastery; learn the local enemies; know which weapons and enemies are stronger against each other.
- If you think a battle cannot be won, flee.
- Build your characters' strength and choose carefully which spells to improve.
- The monastery is dark and items on the floor are hard to find; watch Koudelka's gestures, she indicates the areas that hold items.
- Put magic-user characters in the back row.
- You can fully restore HP and MP at saves; temporary saves do not do this.
- Look at the items in a room and examine them for clues.
- Unconscious characters can be revived with Whiskey and Roman Nuts.
- Get Koudelka's spells to level 3 as soon as possible and have as many spells as possible.

Using Bonus AP to improve characters:
- Bonus AP raise attributes; specialize characters for best results.
- After each battle won, each character gets 4 points to divide.
- Always put 1 point into AGL, as it makes characters faster; it is good for everyone.
- Koudelka's INT and MND should go up so she can use powerful spells.
- Raise Edward's STR, VIT and DEX so he becomes much stronger.
- James is the most flexible: a strong fighter and a good magic user, so you decide what to specialize him in.

Attributes:
- STR (Strength): raises the power of physical attacks.
- VIT (Vitality): raises your life energy (HP).
- DEX (Dexterity): determines the character's aim and agility to dodge a blow.
- AGL (Agility): the number of turns the character gets in a battle; also makes the character faster.
- INT (Intelligence): raises spell power, making spells stronger.
- PIE (Piety): determines max MP and defense against magic.
- MND (Mind): improves the character's aim (with magic).
- LUC (Luck): influences enemy encounters, chance to hit, defense, probability of escaping a fight, etc.
