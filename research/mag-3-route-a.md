## End of Part 1 (tail) [A p.25]
- After the previous boss dies, the party receives the Geyser magic and the Icons Necklace. The guide says Koudelka's and James's magic powers become important soon.
- Item picture: "Pipe" found (screenshot caption).
- James O'Flaherty: talk to him and he is saved. The Holy Water Font then restores the party and works as a save point.
- To continue: return via the kitchen, go up to the 2nd-floor corridor, and take the door at the end where Listel was found earlier.

## PART 2 - Inside the Torture Chamber [A p.25]

### The Hen House
- Take the door opposite and go straight into the Hen House. Collect the pistol ammo, then go straight through the door ahead. You come out in the 2nd-floor corridor, where you can temporarily save.

### 2nd Floor, Corridor
- Walk to the end of the corridor and trigger the cutscene. Guards (monastery creatures) fire on the group; James cannot accept that these creatures could do this.
- After the scene, go to the end of the corridor and try to open the door. You are attacked by three Viewer bosses.

### Viewer Trio Bosses
- Three Viewers, each with different tactics. Colour-to-weakness mapping as the guide gives it:
  - Blue Viewer: any firearm, including Flare magic or the flaming ax.
  - Red Viewer: Geyser magic. The guide says the red one is surely the strongest.
  - Green Viewer: physical attacks (the party does not have Earth magic yet).
- The guide says the fight is hard: be persistent, heal several times. The Viewers focus their magic attacks on Koudelka.
- Reward: items from the Viewers and the Tornado magic (guide: "ganhar itens de batalha deles e receberá a magia Tornado"). Then go through the door they were guarding.

### 2nd Floor, Dungeon Passage
- You can save temporarily here.
- Enter, take the left wall passage: it leads to the prison cell. After this point monsters respawn tougher. You can return to the Herb Garden to save if you wish.

### 2nd Floor, Jail Cell
- Collect Listel and enter the passage to the left. There is a chest needing a combination. The code is not found until you visit the Priests Quarters on the 1st floor. The code is 7038; inside the chest is a diary (screenshot caption: "Guard's Diary"). Then pass the door near the chest and watch another cutscene.
- (Order uncertain: the guide tells the reader where the code comes from before the player gets there.)

### 2nd Floor, The Red Dressing Room
- Koudelka learns the monastery was a prison specialised in killing and torturing its occupants. After the film, go to the wardrobe to the right of the door. Opening the door triggers the Mummified Bride fight.

### Mummified Bride (boss)
- Edward and James use physical attacks; Koudelka attacks with magic. Use Tornado on her.
- About 1000 HP. Her magic is not very effective against the party.
- Reward: Megalith magic.
- After the fight, go back to the wardrobe and open it: inside is a hand ladder (guide: "escada de mão"), needed to descend to the 1st floor.
- Then go back via the cell to the corridor and to the top of the screen. Cutscene: a little ghost girl; Edward nearly dies following her but is saved by Koudelka again. After the scene, go to the end of the corridor and use the hand ladder to descend to the 1st floor.

### 1st Floor, Passage
- Collect Dried Food and ammo for the pistol; they are to the right of the stone on the left side of the passage. Enter the door next to the passage.

### 1st Floor, Priests Quarters
- Enter and pick up the Red Glass Part from the floor. Investigating the altar triggers a battle against ghostly reflections of the party.
- The guide calls it a very hard battle; bring lots of healing magic. It is tedious but you will eventually wipe them out. Tip: spend time going back through explored rooms and fight as many battles as you can first. This gives battle experience and raises magic and weapon levels.
- After the demons: collect Icons Earrings and receive the Revive magic.
- Since you already have the chest code, you do not need the Red Glass for the window (the guide says so; reason not clear, see Unclear).
- Save the game at the altar, watch the film, enter the door to the right of the altar. Watch the film and go through the door to the right of the altar (as printed), then fall into a cell in the basement.

## PART 3 - Trapped in the Dungeon of Horrors [A p.26]

### Basement 1st Floor, Jail Cell
- You land in a cell full of corpses. Go left and take Pistol Rounds and the Antidote from the corpse fallen against the wall.
- Check the prison bars: you cannot escape. Charlotte talks and says you are condemned to stay here forever.
- A Nobleman boss appears after Charlotte's line. Method: Koudelka and James use Flare magic on the demon, Edward attacks with a weapon. (This guide passage also describes a variant where Edward hits the "false Edward", Koudelka hits the "false Koudelka" with Megalith, and James hits the "false James" with Megalith; this wording seems to belong to the Priests Quarters reflection fight. The column layout makes the assignment uncertain.)
- If your skill levels are built up, it takes about 5 minutes and you barely need to heal.
- When the Nobleman dies, a wall falls and you can leave the cell.

### Basement 1st Floor, Torture Room
- Walk to the right wall and find a pair of Knuckles on the floor (caption shows an item pickup prompt).
- Collect the Mace from the table behind the table with the mutilated women's bodies, at the back left of the room.
- Climb the steps and enter the corridor. On the floor are two dolls with labels "Valna" and "Vigna". Between them is a Green Key.
- Enter the double doors on the right wall that lead to the Underground Tunnel.

### Basement 1st Floor, Underground Tunnel
- Walk through the tunnel and check the box near the corpses; inside is Whisky, used to revive a character knocked unconscious in battle.
- Go to the door at the end of the tunnel and enter: you arrive at the Acid Tank room.

### Basement 1st Floor, Acid Room
- Go right to the acid tank. You need a container to carry acid out of the tank, so you must return later.
- Go left to the door of the Underground Tunnel. Collect Pistol Rounds in front of you.
- Return to the door and walk right to the blue double doors. They are destroyed and you cannot enter. Go back to the metal bridge and walk straight to the centre of the screen. Go left and enter Shrine. Walk to the icon on the floor in the centre of the room: this starts the battle with the Larval Human Fly.

### Basement 1st Floor, The Shrine, Larval Human Fly (boss) [B / p.27]
- A huge cybernetic mutation with several spells. Lots of HP and MP, so attack it with earth-based weapons (guide wording).
- Koudelka and James at the back of the group, casting Megalith. Put Edward in front with a sharp weapon.
- Edward attacks continuously with the sword; Koudelka and James bombard with Megalith until the boss's MP is drained (the guide says to drain "o MP de sua equipe"; likely means the boss's, uncertain). Use High Listel to restore MP.
- If it is too hard, gain some levels first.
- Reward: Icons Ring (on its death). Then go to the Holy Fountain and save.
- Screenshot caption shows the Bowgun pickup ("You found Bowgun").

### Basement 1st Floor, Corridor [B / p.27]
- Return to the Acid Tank area where you got the Pistol Rounds. Follow the huge corridor through the door to a room full of treasures.

### Basement 1st Floor, Storage Room [B / p.27]
- After the film, collect the Bowgun, Bowgun Arrows and Pistol Rounds. Leave by the left door in the upper-left corner of the room.

### Basement 1st Floor, Stairs [B / p.27]
- On the wall near the door you came from there is a plaque. Climb the stairs to the next floor.

### 1st Floor, Stairs [B / p.27]
- Go to the corner ahead and read the magic symbols on the wall. Take the High Listel in front and leave by the door in front.

### 1st Floor, Warehouse (first visit) [B / p.27]
- Collect the Stone Tablet on the back wall, then go to the table. Take the Lion Statue and a Letter inside the teddy bear (guide: "ursinho"). Before leaving by the main door you can gain a little experience near the table.
- Raise your Tornado magic: needed for the next boss. Koudelka and James should have Tornado at level 3 before the fight.

### 1st Floor, Shrine, Dark Young (boss) [B / p.27]
- Koudelka senses something is wrong on entering. Go to the altar and prepare for the fight.
- James and Koudelka stay behind, using Tornado. Edward stays in front with an axe and attacks.
- The Dark Young has about 3000 HP. Reward: Evil Horn. Then go to the Holy Water Font and save.

## PART 4 - Koudelka Finds Elaine [B / p.27-28]

### Basement 1st Floor, Stairs
- Go back to where you saw the Goat Statue on the plaque on the wall. (Whether the Goat Statue is picked up here is not stated clearly; see Unclear.)

### 1st Floor, Shrine
- Enter the door to the right of the shrine to reach the warehouse.

### 1st Floor, Warehouse (chandelier)
- When you enter, a chandelier falls near your group. Go to the chandelier and pick up St. Daniel's Arm.

### 2nd Floor, Warehouse Passage
- Climb the stairs: a man with a gun starts shooting. Use Edward's ax to clear the obstacles.
- Koudelka and James should use weapons, not magic, to help Edward. Once obstacles are cleared, Edward can attack the man with a weapon. Three hits finish him.
- Take Brown Glass and Red Key from the spirit. After the fight, go to the man and press X to talk; a short film plays.
- Go back down the stairs and enter the door in the top right of the room. Pass the door. You return to the Straw Storage of the Caretaker's Quarters on the 2nd floor. Walk all the way back to the Living Room.
- Screenshot shows a battle screen with Koudelka, Edward, James.

### 1st Floor, Living Room
- Use the Red Key to enter. Take the Dragon Statue from the table. Take the Badge from the wardrobe and the Rifle Ammo from the shelf. Enter the door to your right.

### 1st Floor, Lumber Room
- Go to the painting on the wall and watch the short sequence. Take the Valna Doll from the table [p.28 start]. Go to the right of the room and take the Mask. Leave and return to the corridor where the chandelier fell. Go back to the treasure room where the Bowgun was found. In the Underground Storage Room go to the right-hand door that was locked earlier.

### Basement 1st Floor, T. Hideaway (Treasure Hideaway; abbreviated in the guide)
- Take the Music Box at the top left of the room. Go to the boxes and read the magic symbols engraved on them. Return to where you fought the thief and enter the door near him.

### 2nd Floor, Showroom (symbol puzzle)
- Enter and look at the magic symbols on the floor. Walk first on the red symbols that were marked on the box.
- First path: starts at the triangle and ends at the "IV".
- Then walk over the four symbols seen on the wall.
- Second path: starts with the symbol that looks like a jug/bottle and ends in the circle with a cross.
- You must walk over all icons to unlock the door and leave. (The exact full order of the intermediate symbols is not stated in the guide.)

### 2nd Floor, Library
- Follow the corridor and enter the door at the end. Pick up the Ochre Glass from the table. Leave the room and return through the corridor. Go to the door that was closed.

### 1st Floor, Study (first visit)
- Go down the stairs and take the Rifle Ammo near the clock. Go close to the door and enter the room, picking up the High Potion near the shelves.

### 1st Floor, Printing Room
- On entering, a film shows Elaine appearing for the first time. Elaine's voice is the one that called Koudelka to the monastery.
- Go to the paintings and place the Stone Tablet on them. The wall falls, revealing a new map of the monastery.

### 1st Floor, Secret Passage
- Enter the newly revealed door and take the record (disc) on top of the gramophone. Take Bowgun Arrows before leaving the room. Exit via the secret passage to the top of the room and enter it.

### 1st Floor, Wine Cellar
- Take the Rifle Rounds and the Blue Glass Part and leave through the door in front of you. Place the Lion, Goat and Dragon statues into the holes in the door to open it. Enter the next room and prepare for a powerful boss.

### 1st Floor, Archive, Chimera (boss)
- Go to the statue on the left wall; the Chimera battle starts. Use Geyser magic with Koudelka and James. About 4000 HP. Use the Rifle with Edward.
- Reward: Icons Crow.
- Enter the fountain and look at the plant ahead; another film starts. After it, go back to the secret passage behind the demolished wall and take the door at the bottom right of the triangular corridor.

### 1st Floor, Triangular Hallway
- You cannot get past the gate, so go left to reach the Church Nave.

### 1st Floor, Church Left Nave
- Go to the centre of the room and pick up the Relief Piece from the floor. Now go back to the clock. (Screenshot caption reads like "there are pieces missing from the stained glass".)

### 1st Floor, Study (second visit)
- Go to the clock and climb up its right side. At the top, place the Relief Piece in the hole in the mural. This opens a new door; enter.

### 2nd Floor, Angel Room (Music Box puzzle)
- On entering, the Music Box starts playing. You must recreate the sound by stepping on the symbols in front of you.
- Order: symbol on the right, then left, then the top one, then the bottom one. This opens a door at the top of the room; enter the next room.
- Throw away the Music Box afterwards.

## PART 4 continued / PART 5 setup [C / p.29-30]

### 2nd Floor, Rogers Room
- Roger Bacon appears and talks a little. Take the Green Glass Part from the floor (the glass is in pieces); you now have all the glass pieces. Return to the Church, to the Left Nave Stained Glass Room on the 1st floor.

### 1st Floor, Church, Left Nave, Stained Glass Room
- Enter the door to the left of the Nave. Fit all the glass pieces into the broken window. Press X to do this; a door opens.
- You also receive the combination for the Library Office chest/safe.
- Leave to the Nave and enter the door at the top of the screen.

### 1st Floor, Church Nave
- Go toward the blue light. When close, an enormous plant stops you from going to the Sanctuary. Go to the end of the Nave and watch the film.
- James and Edward escape the huge demon, but Koudelka is trapped. Do not try to fight it: it is too powerful. Press the O button to flee. Koudelka is now alone.
- Go away from the fight, save, and the game changes to CD 3. (Exact wording of "go away from the game" is slightly garbled; see Unclear.)

## PART 5 - In the Hell of the Hartmans [C / p.29-30]

### 1st Floor, Inner Grounds, Patrick's Quarters
- Go to the woman statue and place the Icons Ring on it. Go to the end of the garden and put the Icons Necklace on the other statue.
- Return to where you entered this courtyard and place the Icons Earring on the statue to your left.
- Go to the fountain and take the stairs on the right. Save. Turn left from the fountain and walk to the statue of a woman. Place the Icons Crow there.
- Return to the fountain and take the Pendant (caption "You found a Pendant"). You cannot finish the game without the Pendant.
- The fountain has been turned off. Go through the door to the right of the fountain and take a High Listel. Now go to the other side of the fountain and enter the small building.
- Screenshot caption: "You can see a door under the water."

### 1st Floor, Arbor
- A guillotine stands at the centre of the room. Something evil lives here. Take the Pistol Rounds at the back of the room. Go left of the guillotine and descend the stairs.

### Basement, Arbor
- Lots of blood. Koudelka is suddenly struck from behind and faints. Later she wakes tied to the guillotine, with Ogden bragging about his axe.
- Ogden's wife (Bessy) appears from nowhere and kills her husband with the axe, saving Koudelka. Listen to Bessy's story; later she kills herself to join her dead husband.
- Take the Rifle RD near the body. Go to Bessy's body and take the Blue Key.
- Return to the dungeon and take the Rifle Rounds on the floor. Go to the altar above and take the bottle of blood (this is the Bottle of Blood used later).
- Go to the dungeon door: it is locked and cannot be opened. Edward appears on the other side of the room. You arrange to meet in the Library. Cross the room to the opposite side; there is a door, go through it.

### Basement 1st Floor, Underground Corridor
- Pass through and enter the door at the end. Prepare for a boss. Use Tornado on this boss since it is very strong.

### Basement 1st Floor, Morsoleum, Gug (boss)
- Koudelka fights Gug alone. Equip Koudelka with Evil Horn, Air Badge and Brooch. With these items you can win if the two Tornados are at level 3.
- Gug has about 5000 HP. When he dies the water flows again and you can save. Exit by the left door.

### Basement 1st Floor, Corridor
- Go to the statue on the left wall. Give it your Bottle of Blood. The statue shakes and reveals a passage. [C / p.30]

### Basement 1st Floor, Altar of Darkness
- Go to the wall in front of you; a creature talks to you. It gives you 16 hints; you only need to talk to it several times to learn the clues. After collecting all hints, leave the room and return to the corridor.
- Take the Roman Nuts in the corridor, then go through the door at the top of the screen.

### Basement 1st Floor, Underground Corridor (return)
- Return to the metal bridge. Take the Pistol Rounds on the floor. Climb the stairs on the opposite side and you come out in the Graveyard.

### Graveyard
- Walk all the way left to find St. Daniel's grave. Pray there and take the Vigna Doll that is to the left of his tomb.
- When you turn, Roger Bacon appears and opens the Triangular Underground Gate.
- Pass through the gate and go to Charlotte's tomb, in the cavern full of stones at the entrance. Pray in front of this tomb; the guide says this prevents a fight with Charlotte later.
- Return to the Library, where you find your two companions. Once reunited, go back to the office on the 2nd floor.

## PART 6 - Patrick and the Emigre Document [C / p.30]

### Patrick's Quarters (heading) / 2nd Floor, Library Office
- Team reunited again. Return to the office on the second floor. Go to the chest that was previously locked. You can now open the chest and read the letters inside.
- Then go back to the Underground Dungeon area. The best route is through the room where the chandelier fell. Save along the way.

### Basement 1st Floor, Dungeon
- Return to where you saw the bodies of two girls. Give them their dolls (Valna Doll and Vigna Doll) and they give you a Green Key. Enter the door near the girls using the Green Key and throw the key away.
- Screenshot shows the ghost-girl figure.

### Basement 1st Floor, D. Stairs
- Go to the left side of the room and take a Potion. Take the stairs to the next floor. Take another Potion here and go through the small door to Charlotte's Cell.

### 1st Floor, Dungeon, Charlotte's Cell (furniture battle)
- Walk past the dining table and prepare for a hard battle. The room begins to vibrate and the furniture starts attacking.
- It is not ordinary furniture: each piece has about 400 HP. Use Megalith on the shelves and Geyser on the chairs.
- After the battle, a film starts. After the talk with Charlotte, go to the centre of the room and take the Flare Brooch. Leave by the door in the corner of the room.
- Screenshot caption: "You found a hidden drawer. Inside it is a book titled Research Notes." (placement near this section; the text elsewhere says Research Notes come from the wardrobe in the Gramophone Room).
- Boss picture (flaming creature, HP number about 4104 seen on screen) appears near this section.

### Basement 1st Floor, D. Passage
- Walk through the passage; on reaching the corpses go right. Here you can take Listel and Pistol Rounds. Climb to the door and use the Blue Key on it. When you pass this door throw the Blue Key away.

### 1st Floor, Patrick's Quarters
- Ignore the stairs and go left to the door. Pick up Shotgun Shells on the way. Enter the door and you go to the Gramophone Room.

### 1st Floor, Patrick's Quarters, Gramophone Room
- Go to the left of the gramophone and put the record on it. Take the Research Notes from the wardrobe. After reading the notes, go to the door at the end of the room and enter.

### 1st Floor, Patrick's Quarters, Portrait Room, Scapegoat (boss)
- Another boss battle; you face Scapegoat. Keep Edward close to the boss while James and Koudelka stay away. Koudelka and James use Flare magic.
- When Edward takes lots of damage, James should heal him. The boss has about 6000 HP. The guide text continues past the last page provided (after "quando ..."). Not available.
