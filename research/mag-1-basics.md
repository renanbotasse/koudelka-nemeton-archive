# Magazine guide pp.26-29: basics, controls, menus, battle, characters, demon bosses

Sources: "guide-01" = p.26; "guide-02" = p.27; "guide-03" = pp.28-29 (two-page spread). All facts paraphrased in English.

## Intro / premise [p.26]
- Setting: Halloween (31 Oct 1898, a Monday), Nemeton Abbey, Aberystwyth, Wales.
- Koudelka Iasant is drawn to the abbey by the voice of a weeping woman. The abbey was built in the 9th century by an Irish saint, Daniel Scotius, as an isolated place to keep evil spirits and monsters contained.
- Inside she finds a dying man, Edward J. Plunkett, who came after hearing a London rumor of money and women at the abbey.
- The old monastery was later occupied by Patrick Heyworth, an eccentric dilettante who used it for satanic experiments.
- Koudelka heals Edward and he joins her. She then saves James O'Flaherty, a Vatican bishop sent by the Pope to find a secret historical document; he also joins to uncover the truth about the abbey.
- They fight many evil spirits and monsters. Later the party learns Nemeton had become a prison where inmates were tortured, mutilated and killed.
- Genre: RPG-adventure; foes include demons, floating furniture. Characters are built up using bonus points.

## Controls [p.26]
- Four game screens: Event, Field, Battle, Menu. Action takes place on Field. The Menu is used to change party formation and equip items.
- Triangle: run; also cancels X commands.
- X: attack enemies, climb/descend stairs, confirm selections of weapons/items, open doors, pick up items.
- Circle: flee from battle; open the menu.
- START: skip cutscenes.
- L1/R1: select a character in the menu.
- D-pad: move character direction.

## Menu commands [pp.26-27]
- Circle in the field opens the menu. Commands: Heal, Status, Equip, Items, Map, Formation, Read, Config (last entry partly unclear, see Unclear).
- Heal: use healing magic on a party member; a character at full HP has their name shown in gray. [p.26]
- Status: per-character screen, L1/R1 to switch. Top shows level, HP, MP and equipment. Bottom shows stats, experience and experience needed for next level. Three other sub-screens (reached with left/right) show known magic with level and experience per spell, and per-weapon experience. Triangle returns. [p.26]
- Equip: arm characters with weapons, armor, accessories. R1/L1 switches character, X opens the slot's item list, up/down to browse, right shows item description and stat change. X confirms, Triangle goes back. [p.26]
- Items: four categories: Weapon, Armor, Accessory, Tool. Sub-commands: Use (use an item), Discard (remove an item), Rename (rename an object), Examine (short description). [p.26]
- Map: up/down changes floor. Visited rooms are marked; rooms where an event happened get an X mark; save points are marked with a red symbol; your position blinks. The map reveals more as you go deeper into the monastery. [p.27]
- Formation: set the party's starting positions for battle. Characters are chess pieces: Koudelka = queen, Edward = knight, James = bishop. L1/R1 selects character and X picks up the piece, D-pad moves it, X again places it. Triangle returns. [pp.26-27]
- Read: read letters, books, any written material found. [p.27]

## Saving [p.27]
- Stepping on a Save Point shows a prompt to save. Sometimes you must defeat a monster to reach a save point. X = Save or Cancel.
- Temporary Save: available when an "S" appears at the end of a room description; press Circle for a quick save.

## Battle system [p.27, p.28]
- Turn-based on a chessboard-like grid. Each character performs one action per turn. A grayed battle-menu entry means unusable (out of reach of enemy, or already attacked/moved).
- After battle characters gain experience and may find items. Normal battles are random encounters. Battle experience makes characters stronger and improves magic and weapon proficiency. Fighting demons and evil spirits yields many items and weapons.
- You cannot move through a square occupied by an enemy or another character. Enemies cannot walk past you and vice versa, so you can physically block for weaker characters: put Edward or James in front of Koudelka so she cannot be hit physically.
- Enemy status attacks: poison, paralysis, silence. Poison slowly drains HP; cure with Panacea or Antidote. Paralyzed characters cannot move, cast, or attack and are vulnerable; Panacea cures paralysis. Silence stops casting but not moving/fighting; Panacea cures silence. Status effects are removed automatically at battle end. [p.27]
- Battle commands: Action (submenu: Attack, Magic, Item, Weapon), Move, Wait, Status, Escape.
  - Attack: D-pad cursor selects target, X attacks.
  - Magic: pick a spell from the list with D-pad, X to select, then choose target/area and X to cast.
  - Item: use an item from inventory.
  - Weapon: switch current weapon, but you then cannot attack that turn. Right inspects attributes, X selects.
  - Move: one move per character per turn; range is shown in blue squares; X to confirm.
  - Wait: forfeit the turn.
  - Status: view the character's current status.
  - Escape: press Circle during battle to select escape, X to confirm. Sometimes escape is not possible.
- Poison attacks: cured with certain potions/items; effects may cause abnormalities and last only during the battle. [p.28]
- Zero Hit Points: character does not die; they become unconscious on the field and wake with 1 HP at battle end. If all three are at 0 HP, game over. [p.28]
- Skill levels: weapons and magic gain skill level with use; higher level means stronger effect. Check in Status screen. [p.28]

## Battle strategy tips [p.28]
- Keep every character acting each turn. If Edward is in front hitting enemies, Koudelka and James use healing or Reflect magic or build up stats.
- Edward in front, Koudelka behind. James is a fighter and caster, so put him in front for melee or behind for spells. Two fighters in front can support each other. If James is unconscious next to Edward, monsters cannot reach him; if James falls behind a demon, you cannot get to him until the demon or battle ends.
- Alternate Edward's weapons so none break. Level up all kinds of Koudelka's magic so she can beat any demon; if she only has Geyser, she will struggle when Tornado is needed.
- Use items and weapons as needed but sparingly. Use Escape when a battle goes badly or you are out of ammo.

## Main characters [p.28]
- Koudelka Iasant: a medium of Gypsy origin, drawn to the abbey by a voice begging for help. Her powers lie in magic and mysticism; strong caster, independent woman.
- Edward J. Plunkett: poorly educated adventurer who came seeking sexual pleasures. Excellent fighter with much physical ability.
- James O'Flaherty: Vatican bishop secretly sent by the Pope to find a lost document. Fanatic about the Bible and seems to hide something. Powerful fighter and excellent caster.
- Ogden and Bessy Hartman: abbey caretakers; offer hospitality and hot soup, but the soup is poisoned.
- Roger Bacon: wizard living at the abbey since the 13th century, survived through magical powers via the document Emigre. Gives valuable information.
- Charlotte: imprisoned since birth, never saw daylight. Abandoned by her mother, wants revenge on all living things. Proud of pain, misery and suffering; truly demonic.
- Patrick Heyworth: eccentric owner of the abbey. Not seen in the game, but his depraved lifestyle's remnants are found in the corridors.
- Elaine Heyworth: Patrick's wife, killed 20 years ago. Her voice drew Koudelka to the abbey. Found in spirit and flesh; spirit form is friendly, her fleshly form is pure evil. Fought in the final battle to save Nemeton Abbey.

## Demon bosses [p.29] (hint and weakness)
- Werewolf: not hard; a pistol finishes them easily. First demon you face.
- Tree Demon: must kill to rescue James. Arm Edward with a blade for physical attacks; Koudelka uses Flare to burn. High HP, so be persistent; poison attacks can poison party.
- Viewer Trio: use magic. Red Viewer: Geyser. Blue Viewer: Flare. Green Viewer: any weapon. If magic is slow, have Edward hit them with weapons. Located along the dungeon corridor and guards' quarters.
- Mummified Bride: send her back to the spirit realm to get the rope ladder to descend into the dungeon. Use Flare; she is very fast, and attacks with magic and physical hits. Located in a wardrobe.
- Reflections: in the priests' quarters, a trio of ghost reflections of the party. First attack the fake James and Edward, ignoring Koudelka. Koudelka and Edward attack fake Edward with magic while Edward hits fake James with a heavy weapon. When both are dead they attack fake Koudelka. Advisable to spend time building magic levels first.
- Larval Human Fly: powerful magic, serious HP damage. Use Megalith to destroy it. Found via a genetic mutation at the Shrine below the monastery. If magic is slow, Edward attacks with any weapon.
- Dark Young: very fast. Dispatch with Tornado; it attacks before you act. Edward attacks with magic or a weapon; Koudelka casts Tornado while James cures Edward's damage. Found at the Holy Water Font.
- Human Hydra: three cobra heads as neck, prey on its body. Use Geyser. James and Koudelka cast magic to kill quickly. Found in the library building.
- Gug: eyeless monster that attacks with magic and heavy physical weight. Fight in its own underground passage. Use Tornado; must hit with at least level 3 magic to kill.
- Charlotte: if you have Sophias' cards and pray at Charlotte's tomb, you may avoid the fight. If you must fight, use Geyser. She hits hard with magic; try to avoid. Found in her dungeon cell.
- Scapegoat: many legs jumping everywhere. Easy to kill; use Flare. It attacks the nearest person, with magic. Edward is the physical fighter; James heals Edward while Koudelka attacks with Flare.
- Apostle: a problem; do NOT use physical attacks (they strengthen it). Hangs from ceiling by a leg and casts on the party. Cast Reflect on the whole party to protect them. Do not cast magic when it is trembling since that only heals it. Hit it with magic during its dance periods; cannot attack when it is dancing. Waits at the Vestry Chapel.
- Gargoyle: most effective demon in the abbey; heavy damage, magic effective on whole party. Use Reflect to protect the party against its magic. Use lots of healing magic to avoid unconsciousness. Koudelka and James approach with many magics, all work on it. Edward attacks with Lifedrinker. Not easy to destroy.
- Dissected Tentacles: lots of HP, powerful magic. Found when you reach the sanctuary to face Elaine. Use Flare to burn these mutilated things; they are no problem now that the magic should be strong. Flare works very well.
- Elaine: appears twice, when climbing the abbey spire. First fight use Flare, second Tornado to damage her tormented soul. Immune to physical attacks, so no point trying. Second fight a bit easier since she is hurt. Edward hits with Evil Horn while Koudelka and James cast Geyser and Megalith. Hits sometimes and she falls.
- Perfect Elaine: final phase; she will not return to the grave easily. Evil, dangerous and extremely lethal. Use Megalith to finish her. Arm Edward with a Lifedrinker sword. Use Reflect to protect the party. Koudelka and James cast magic on Elaine while Edward attacks with a Lifedrinker.
