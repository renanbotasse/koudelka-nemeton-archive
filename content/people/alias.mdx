Alias is the name only the credits give him. He is an immigrant thief hiding in Nemeton's storage rooms, and he is the one person on the estate who has seen what the house does.

<Seal level="story">

He tries to take the guests' valuables and is caught. What he saw tells the party about the caretakers, and his reward for telling it is a quick, unwatched death.

</Seal>


<Seal level="story">

**Boss notes.** Magic always misses him, so fight with physical attacks. Barrels stacked in front of him must be broken first. A ranged weapon helps. *Single play-through report, medium-low confidence.*

</Seal>
