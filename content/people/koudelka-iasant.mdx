She arrives at the monastery on All-Hallows' Eve, 1898, drawn by a voice calling her name.

A medium by gift and an exile by sentence, she carries no home with her. What she carries is a habit of listening, and Nemeton has a great deal to say.

## What the file holds

Her closest companions on the night are the adventurer Edward Plunkett and the bishop James O'Flaherty. Neither arrived for the same reason she did.

<EditorialNote>

Koudelka is a Romani woman. The game and many fan wikis call her by a different word, which is an old exonym and is often used as an insult. This archive writes *Romani* (adjective) and *Roma* (the people), and keeps the other word only inside quotations from the game, unaltered, so the source stays honest. Nothing here invents Romani culture for her: what the game states about her is what the file records.

</EditorialNote>

## Where the name comes from

The name came from the photographer Josef Koudelka, whom the game's director liked for how it sounded, with no obvious origin attached. *(Source: Kikuta's own site, via Wikipedia.)*
