The real Roger Bacon (c. 1219–c. 1292) was an English Franciscan friar who taught at Oxford and Paris, argued for observation and experiment, and sent his *Opus Majus* to the pope in 1267. He is also, by long tradition, a magician, which has done him no favours with historians.

In the game he is alive in 1898, and the reason is not good. He lies in a coffin behind the library shelves, thin as paper and oddly cheerful, and he knows exactly what the book is and who is looking for it. He is the only character who appears in every game of the series.

## Fact and fiction

Everything about his age in 1898 belongs to the game. The medieval dates above are history. The archive keeps the two apart.
