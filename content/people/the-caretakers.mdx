Ogden and Bessy Hartman are the old couple who run Nemeton. They greet visitors with soup, with tidy rooms and, if asked, with ammunition. They seem kind. A long wall of paintings, all of the same ship, is the first sign that kindness is not the whole of them.

## Why the paintings

The ship is the *Princess Alice*, a real steamer that sank in the Thames in 1878. See [file 006](/archive/files/the-princess-alice) for the history, and for where the game departs from it.

<Seal level="story">

Ogden was not only a hired hand. In the game's fiction he is the captain who lost the *Princess Alice*, and he owed his shelter at Nemeton to the woman of the house. The couple helped the master with the sacrifices that fed his ritual and poisoned strangers who arrived. Their devotion to each other is the engine of everything they do, and it ends with one of them choosing the other over the house.

</Seal>
