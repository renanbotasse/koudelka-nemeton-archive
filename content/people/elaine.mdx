Elaine Heyworth (born Spencer) is never met alive. You meet her as a portrait, a name, a story the other characters tell differently depending on what they lost. Three people in the house loved her, and each remembers someone else.

<Seal level="story">

She is the voice that called Koudelka to Nemeton. Her husband, Patrick, killed hundreds trying to bring her back, and he succeeded only with her body. Her spirit begs to have it burned. The body is the last enemy of the night.

</Seal>


<Seal level="endings">

**Boss notes.** Three forms. The first is weak to fire and wind; the second to water and earth, and to physical attacks. Reflect is essential. Missing her keepsake can lead to the bad ending. *Single play-through report, medium-low confidence.*

</Seal>
