Charlotte is a ghost, a girl of nine who was held at Nemeton some three hundred years before the night of the story. She is cruel to the guests, because she was left. She appears early, she laughs when people fall, and she is not easy to like.

<Seal level="story">

She is the unacknowledged daughter of a noblewoman called Sophia D'Lota and a man named Phillip Christopher, hidden in Wales to keep a scandal quiet. Her mother wrote to her, and none of the letters were ever delivered. Whether she is freed or fought depends on whether the player finds them.

</Seal>

<EditorialNote>

Her mother's name and the castle in her letters echo a real woman: Sophia Dorothea of Celle (1666–1726), confined for thirty years after an affair. The game borrows the outline. The real Sophia never had a daughter called Charlotte.

</EditorialNote>


<Seal level="story">

**Boss notes.** She is only fought if her story is left unresolved. Water magic does the most damage. If you resolve it, there is no fight, and the reward differs. *Single play-through report, medium-low confidence.*

</Seal>
