Edward Plunkett is young, well-read and armed. He comes to Nemeton looking for something to write about, which is rarely a safe reason to enter a monastery.

## Marginal note

The name is shared with the real Edward Plunkett, 18th Baron of Dunsany, a writer born in 1878. The game borrows the name. The file keeps the two apart.
