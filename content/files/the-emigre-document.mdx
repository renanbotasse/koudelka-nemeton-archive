The Emigre Document is an invention of the game. No manuscript of that name exists. In the story it is a book of Celtic and Fomorian origin on resurrection and endless life, bound in the shape of a skull, copied and recopied and always on its way to someone new. *Émigré* is French for emigrant, and the book never stays.

## Provenance (as fiction)

Passed hand to hand, copied by a friar, sought by the Vatican and bought by a man with a grief to heal. The chain of custody is the plot.

## A real echo

Fans often compare it with the Voynich manuscript, a real, undeciphered 15th-century book that tradition once attributed to the same Roger Bacon who appears in the game.
