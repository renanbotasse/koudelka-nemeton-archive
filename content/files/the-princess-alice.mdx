On 3 September 1878 the pleasure steamer *Princess Alice* sank in the Thames at Gallions Reach after a collision with the collier *Bywell Castle*. An estimated 600 to 700 people died; the ship was overcrowded and no passenger list was kept. The event was among the worst peacetime losses in British river history.

## What the game does with it

*Koudelka* turns the wreck into the backstory of one of its characters, who claims to have been its captain. This is a licence taken by the fiction. The real captain, William Grinstead, died in the disaster.

The archive treats the disaster as history first. If the portraits in the house look strange, that is why.
